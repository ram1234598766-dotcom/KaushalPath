01 / Study hub

Make a difficult topic clearer.

Choose a class, write the subject and chapter, then ask for notes or an explanation. You can enter languages, electives and vocational subjects too.

For an exact textbook-aligned explanation, mention the chapter and edition or paste a short excerpt you are allowed to share.
The AI can try to read public pages you specify. Sign-in pages, paywalls, video pages and blocked documents may not be readable.

AI responses can be wrong. Compare important facts with your prescribed textbook and official syllabus.

AI and web-grounding availability depends on the Netlify deployment and AI Gateway configuration. The rest of this study site works without AI.
