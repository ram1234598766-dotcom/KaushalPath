# KaushalPath India

**Seva First Challenge · Shikshit & Kaushal Bharat**

## Why this project?

Every student has questions. Every student deserves a way forward.

School can feel overwhelming when a chapter does not make sense, an exam is approaching, or a student is unsure what to learn next. Sometimes a student needs the same idea explained in simpler words, full chapter notes, a worked example, or a place to ask a question without feeling embarrassed. Finding the right chapter and knowing what to revise can be difficult too.

At the same time, education is about more than remembering answers. Students are also discovering what they enjoy, how they solve problems, how they communicate, and what useful skills they might build for the future. Not every learner discovers those strengths in the same way or at the same time.

KaushalPath India was imagined as a friendly learning companion: one place to return to when a topic feels difficult, an interest begins to grow, or a student simply needs a next step.

## What does it do?

KaushalPath India brings CBSE learning and skill discovery together. Students choose a class, subject and chapter, then use an AI guide to explore notes, understand a confusing idea, revise important points or practise with questions.

### 1. Chapter-wise learning for Classes 10–12

Students choose a class and subject, ask the guide to find official chapter lists, and request original notes for a chapter or topic. Notes can include key ideas, definitions, worked examples, common mistakes, practice questions and revision points. The subject selection follows the student's own school syllabus.

### 2. An AI guide that points back to sources

When configured, the guide searches official CBSE and NCERT pages and displays reference links. Students can ask follow-up questions, request a revision sheet, practise with an answer key, or add a short textbook excerpt for context. AI can make mistakes, so important details should be checked against the prescribed textbook and teacher guidance.

### 3. Discover practical strengths, one step at a time

A short reflection helps students notice whether they enjoy problem-solving, digital creation, communication, creative work, or helping their community. It suggests a small activity they can try, while a simple progress area helps them keep track of their own learning steps. These suggestions are starting points, not labels or career verdicts.

The aim is simple: help students feel more confident asking questions, understanding lessons, and taking the next step in their own learning.
