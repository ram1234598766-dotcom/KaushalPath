# KaushalPath India

**Seva First Challenge · Shikshit & Kaushal Bharat**

## Why this project?

Every student has questions. Every student deserves a way forward.

School can feel overwhelming when a chapter does not make sense, an exam is approaching, or a student is unsure what to revise. Sometimes a student needs the same idea explained in simpler words, a worked example, or a patient explanation of one confusing step. Finding the correct chapter and knowing what to study next can take time away from learning itself.

Study material can also become scattered. Notes may be in one place, practice questions in another, and curriculum information somewhere else. A learner may understand a lesson in class but struggle when trying to apply it alone at home. Some hesitate to ask the same question more than once because they worry about slowing the class down or being judged.

KaushalPath India is imagined as a friendly learning companion: one place to return to when a topic feels difficult, revision feels overwhelming, or a student needs a clearer explanation. It aims to make it easier to begin studying, ask focused questions, and continue until an idea makes sense.

## What does it do?

KaushalPath India brings CBSE study support for Classes 10, 11 and 12 into one approachable learning space. Students choose a class, subject and topic, then explore chapter-wise notes, ask the AI guide questions, practise with answers and explanations, and prepare revision material.

### 1. Chapter-wise learning for Classes 10–12

Students choose their class and subject, find a chapter or enter a specific topic, and request structured notes. Notes can include an overview, key terms, definitions, worked examples, common mistakes, practice questions and revision points. Students should choose the subject combination that matches their own school syllabus.

### 2. An AI guide for specific questions

When configured, the AI guide can explain difficult ideas in simpler language, show steps, compare two concepts, give examples, and answer follow-up questions. Students can ask for explanations suited to their selected class and subject, then compare the answer with the prescribed textbook and their teacher's guidance.

### 3. Revision and practice

Students can request concise summaries, revision sheets, quizzes, and practice questions with answer explanations. A wrong answer should help reveal which concept or step needs another look, rather than being treated as a judgement of the learner.

### 4. Guidance across subjects

Different subjects need different kinds of support. Mathematics and Physics may require step-by-step solutions and formula explanations; Biology may need clear definitions and process descriptions; History and Geography may benefit from timelines and comparisons; languages may need help with grammar, comprehension, themes, or writing structure. Commerce, humanities, computer-related subjects and electives may need their own examples and answer formats.

### 5. Clear sources and honest limits

The guide is intended to point students toward official CBSE curriculum and NCERT textbook resources when available. Its built-in offline lesson collection is a starter library, not a complete copy of every chapter. Broader AI answers require the optional AI service to be configured and internet access. AI can make mistakes, so students should verify important information against the current prescribed syllabus and textbook.

The aim is simple: help students find the right topic, feel comfortable asking questions, understand the explanation, and take a clear next step in their studies.
