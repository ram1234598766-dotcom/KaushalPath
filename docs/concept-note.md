# KaushalPath — Concept Note
## Shikshit & Kaushal Bharat

### Why this project matters
Students in Classes 10, 11 and 12 often need to move between textbooks, notes, practice questions and exam resources. A student who gets stuck on one step may not know where to ask a follow-up question, and students preparing for JEE Main or NEET UG need repeated practice and useful feedback. KaushalPath aims to bring these activities together in one approachable learning space.

### What KaushalPath does
KaushalPath is a browser-based learning website for CBSE-aligned study support across Classes 10–12. Learners can choose a class, enter a subject—including languages, academic electives and vocational subjects—and name a chapter or topic they want to understand. The study guide can generate structured notes, explanations, revision sheets, original practice questions and step-by-step worked solutions when AI service is configured.

### Chapter-wise learning
Students can ask for learning objectives, definitions, formula summaries, examples, common mistakes, recap points and self-check questions. The experience is designed to respond to the topic the learner enters rather than require them to search through a fixed list. The built-in AI output is created on demand; it is not a claim that a complete, human-verified offline note exists for every chapter in every subject.

### Classes 10, 11 and 12
The interface separates these classes so students can state their learning level. It accepts common subjects and permits custom subject names to include school-specific electives and languages. Because subject offerings and syllabi can vary by school, learners should compare the learning plan with their school's current subject list and the published CBSE curriculum.

### JEE Main and NEET UG preparation
The website includes original short practice sets for concept practice, including timed questions, answer review and explanations. The sets help demonstrate the test-taking workflow; they are not full-length official mock exams. Their practice scoring is configurable demonstration logic and must not be treated as a promise of the exact marking scheme for any particular exam year.

### Previous-year questions and free resources
Students can open official CBSE, NCERT and NTA resource portals from the site. Genuine previous-year questions and official answer keys should be sourced from the relevant official portal where available. KaushalPath labels its own bundled sample items as original practice questions and does not falsely label them as authentic PYQs.

### Test results and progress
After a test, students can review answers and explanations and download results as CSV or JSON. The print function can save the result report as a PDF through the browser. The demo stores recent results in local browser storage, so records do not automatically synchronise across different devices or browsers.

### Responsible AI
AI can make mistakes, misread a question or present outdated details. The guide is an assistant for learning, not an official CBSE, NCERT or NTA answer service. Students should verify important information, formulas and exam updates against prescribed textbooks, teacher guidance and official notices. Web-grounded sources may be returned when the AI service supports them, but a link is not a guarantee that every generated statement is correct.

### Expected educational value
KaushalPath seeks to make revision more approachable, reduce the friction of asking follow-up questions, encourage self-testing and help learners identify areas that need additional attention. Its broader goal is to support independent learning and equitable access to useful study pathways.

### Access and limitations
The main practice interface can run as a static website. AI-generated study help requires a deployed Netlify Function and an available Netlify AI Gateway / provider configuration; AI usage can consume account credits. This is a student-facing prototype and should be expanded with expert-reviewed content, accessibility testing, privacy review, and broader test banks before it is relied upon as a comprehensive learning service.
